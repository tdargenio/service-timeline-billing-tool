# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

## EDGE Billing Timeline Assistant Privacy Policy

EDGE Billing Timeline Assistant is designed to assist authorized users with reviewing billing timeline information within supported business applications.

## Information Collection

The extension does not collect, sell, transmit, or share personal information with third parties.

The extension may access information displayed on supported billing and policy pages solely to provide the functionality requested by the user, including:

- Policy numbers
- Billing timeline events
- Billing transaction information
- Page content required to generate timeline summaries

## Data Usage

Data accessed by the extension is used only within the user's browser session to:

- Read billing timeline information
- Organize and summarize timeline events
- Display results within the extension side panel
- Allow users to copy policy identifiers and related information

## Data Storage

The extension may store the following locally within the user's browser:

- User preferences
- Display settings
- Cached extension settings
- Feature configuration options

No accessed billing or policy information is transmitted to external servers by the extension.

## Third-Party Sharing

The extension does not sell, share, rent, transfer, or disclose data to third parties.

## Remote Code

The extension does not download or execute remote code. All functionality is packaged within the extension.

## Security

Data processed by the extension remains within the user's browser environment and is used only to provide the extension's functionality.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any updates will be reflected in the published version of this document.
